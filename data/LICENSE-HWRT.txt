symbols.tpl and inkmlp.bin (handwritten symbol data and classifier, built into techy-notes.exe)

Contains information from the HWRT database of handwritten symbols by Martin Thoma
(https://zenodo.org/records/50022), about 90% of which was collected by Daniel Kirsch for Detexify
(https://github.com/kirel/detexify-data). The HWRT database is made available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

symbols.tpl is a derivative database of it (up to 20 samples per symbol, one per writer, strokes resampled and
scaled into the unit square), made by tools/hwrt2tpl.cpp, and is likewise available under the ODbL v1.0.
inkmlp.bin is a produced work: a neural network trained by tools/hwrt2tpl.cpp on HWRT's training split.
The drawn shapes for = ( ) t were made by the Techy-Notes authors.
